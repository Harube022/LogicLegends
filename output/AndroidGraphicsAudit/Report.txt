Logic Legends — solo graphics audit
10 November 2026

Implemented locally. No commits, pushes, scene replacements, account changes or reward changes.

FINDINGS

1. TruthTable's PT_Grass_Mat uses a legacy transparent foliage shader. Its forward
   pass blends alpha but writes depth, including texels that should be invisible;
   alpha clipping is conditional and is not enabled on the authored grass material.
   It also includes expensive PBR/translucency/wind variants and multiple passes.
   This is a confirmed material/depth defect and a compatibility risk. The exact
   multicolored Android streaks were NOT reproduced on the desktop GPU, so their
   device-specific cause cannot be declared conclusively diagnosed.

2. TruthTable's saved scene contains 1,756 legacy foliage renderers. PRELIM and
   RulesOfInference each contain 1,626, including inactive content. No unsupported
   shaders were reported by the current Editor graphics API in any saved trial.
   These are scene inventories, not counts of objects drawn in each frame.

3. Low previously limited per-object light evaluation and render resolution but
   did not cap active scene-local lights. Authored post-processing could remain
   enabled. Scene lighting scripts could enable lights independently of settings.

4. The late-order settings component could miss first-scene initialization: the
   component existed but its scene graphics host was absent. A Start fallback and
   active-scene-change handling fix this. Installation is idempotent.

CHANGES

- SoloFoliage.shader: lightweight URP alpha-cutout foliage, proper alpha discard
  before depth writes, matching depth-only pass, bounded wind, safe normals and
  float lighting accumulation. Keeps original card geometry, textures, tint and
  coverage settings. Runtime material copies apply only to the legacy plant shader
  in solo trials. Pack source materials and multiplayer assets are unchanged.
- SoloGraphicsBudget / SoloSceneGraphics: shared limits inside the existing solo
  settings implementation. Closest local lights have priority, with hysteresis.
  Light intensity/flicker scripts retain their authored animation; the final gate
  controls cost. Foliage cost reduction changes renderers only, never colliders.
- SoloTrialVisuals: Low caps shadows and post-processing; camera settings are
  enforced after scene scripts. Material/camera/light changes restore on cleanup.
- SoloSettingsRuntime: applies on initial entry, active-scene transitions and reloads,
  without duplicate hosts. Reuses the existing saved preference key and controls.
- Graph bloom respects graphics quality as well as lighting quality.
- Student navigation build slices use smaller Low/Medium CPU budgets. Population,
  routes, safety checks, walk/run speeds, dialogue and hint limits are unchanged.
- Existing minimap rendering already caches its image and throttles updates;
  that behavior was inspected and retained. No physics/gameplay rules were changed.

QUALITY LIMITS

                           Low          Medium          High
Android render scale       0.70         0.90            1.00
Local real-time lights     up to 6      up to 12        up to 24
Grass density              50%          75%             100%
Grass draw distance        65 units     100 units       150 units
Post-processing            off          permitted       permitted
Directional shadows        off          capped Medium   user selection
NPC map build slice        0.5 ms        1 ms            2 ms

Lighting quality can further reduce the light limit. Shadow and motion-blur
preferences are retained even when the graphics ceiling temporarily disables them.
Local-light shadow maps are disabled. Shader repair applies at every quality level;
the additional Low/Medium foliage reduction is a separate performance measure.
The existing mip/LOD settings and saved frame-rate preference remain in use.

MEASUREMENTS

Unity 6000.3.6f1, Windows Editor, NVIDIA GeForce GTX 1070 Ti, Direct3D 12.
Android was the active build target. No Android device/emulator was attached.
Actual saved TruthTable art and sunset lighting in an isolated rendering fixture;
fixed view near its real spawn, 800 x 480 Game View, 90 sampled frames per case
after warm-up. Gameplay/account/reward controllers, NPCs and minimap were excluded.
Therefore these are rendering-only Editor figures, NOT full-trial Android FPS.

Case                 Draws/frame  SetPass/frame  Triangles/frame  Local lights
Before Low (legacy)   370.5        12.0           997,703          25
After Low            277.2        11.0           995,224           6
After Medium         502.0        16.0         1,642,852          12
After High           627.5        16.0         1,927,979          24

Low draw calls decreased about 25.2%; active local lights decreased 76%.
Main-thread samples, including wait/Editor overhead, stayed near 16.5 ms. This
desktop test was frame limited; it does NOT establish an FPS increase or GPU-time
improvement. Device CPU/GPU frame times, thermals and full-game performance remain
unmeasured. Higher tiers intentionally render more detail and lighting.
