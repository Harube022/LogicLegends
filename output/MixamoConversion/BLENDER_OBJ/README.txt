12 unrigged character OBJ packages for Mixamo.
Open Female Skins or Male Skins, then the rarity folder. Upload the *_Mixamo_OBJ.zip inside that folder to include textures.
Each folder also includes the OBJ, MTL, textures, local preview and instructions.
Sources were not modified. Every export was reimported locally and checked; Mixamo uploads were not performed.
