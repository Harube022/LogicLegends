Mixamo Color-Only Upload Packages
Open Female Skins or Male Skins, then the skin folder. Upload its *_Mixamo_ColorOnly.zip directly to Mixamo without extracting it.
Each ZIP contains the unchanged unrigged OBJ, a standard diffuse-only MTL and its base-color texture, all at the archive root.
Normal, roughness and metallic textures are intentionally omitted for this compatibility test. Originals remain in BLENDER_OBJ.
All material names and texture references have been checked locally. Mixamo appearance and auto-rigging have not been tested online.
