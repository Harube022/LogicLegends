Import InductionBoards.unitypackage into Unity 6 with URP and TextMeshPro.
Use Assets/Art/InductionBoards/Prefabs/InformationBoard.prefab and
Assets/Art/InductionBoards/Prefabs/FormulaScrollsStand.prefab.
Both FBXs, baked PNG textures, URP materials and editable labels are included.
All four scrolls share one reusable mesh; ReusableFormulaScroll.prefab is also
provided. The two full assets have bottom-center pivots.
See Assets/Art/InductionBoards/README.txt for texture extraction, the explicit
URP material recipe, setup menu, and label editing instructions.
Blender source, bake/geometry/Unity reports and previews are in SourceAssets.
Unity_Assets_Preview.png shows the actual imported FBXs, materials and text.
No scene, altar or gameplay system is part of this pack.
