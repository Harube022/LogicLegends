Option 3 foliage only: clipped green bushes, pink and white flowers, and purple
lavender-like spikes. No stone edging, ground base, arches or tree is included.
Import Option3Foliage.unitypackage into Unity 6 with URP and use its prefabs.
The main FBX is Assets/Art/Option3Foliage/Option3_BushesAndFlowers.fbx.
Hedge_Segment.fbx and Mixed_Flower_Cluster.fbx are reusable individual pieces.
See Assets/Art/Option3Foliage/README.txt for material setup and resizing.
Blender source and previews are in SourceAssets/Option3Foliage.
