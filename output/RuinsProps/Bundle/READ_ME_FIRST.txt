PALE STONE RUINS PROPS FOR YOUR MAP
Open SourceAssets/RuinsProps/Ruins_Props.blend for the editable Blender scene.
Main FBX: Assets/Art/RuinsProps/Ruins_Props_Perimeter.fbx.
Nine individual prop FBXs are also included for custom placement.
Import RuinsProps.unitypackage into a Unity 6 URP project to use ready-made
prefabs and correctly mapped stone/moss materials.
The map itself is not included or modified. The perimeter's open center is
approximately 8 m x 8 m; adjust the independent props to fit your map.
See Assets/Art/RuinsProps/README.txt for detailed setup and modular contents.
