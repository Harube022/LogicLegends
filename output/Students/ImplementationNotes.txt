ROAMING STUDENTS — LOCAL IMPLEMENTATION

Shared runtime: Assets/Scripts/Scripts/Students
Configuration: Assets/Resources/Students/StudentNpcCatalog.asset
Editor tools: Logic Legends > Students

No scene assignments are required. The runtime installs one scene-local director
only in the ten TrialCatalog solo scenes. Garden, menus and multiplayer are excluded.
The catalog references the twelve existing prepared Gameplay prefabs. It does not
change equipment IDs, ownership, original skins, controllers or animation clips.

POPULATIONS
Propositional Logic: 1–2 in each of the five topic chambers; identities are unique
across the population. Inactive distant chamber students pause their navigation.
Truth Table / Rules of Inference: 2–3, retained between challenges.
Formal / Direct / Induction / Sets / Functions / Trees: random 0, 1 or 2 per challenge.
Graph: 1–2 on generated GraphVertex floors, none in Challenge 2. Question rebuilds
replace Graph NPCs so no student can remain on a deleted platform.
The equipped skin is excluded. Admin character changes replace a conflicting
student skin without changing account equipment or generating duplicate skins.

NAVIGATION AND MOBILE COST
Collision-derived, incremental walking graphs validate ground support, body
clearance, bidirectional segments, water hazards and interaction clearance.
Walk paths use A*. Temporary obstructions block a node briefly and trigger a
new destination. Recent goals are avoided. The larger island maps keep expanding
after students spawn locally; work is sliced across frames. Graph floors use an
explicit GraphVertex whitelist, never a decorative surface or renderer bounds.
Truth Table scans from playable ground with a 1.25-unit grid (30,000-cell cap).
Disconnected porch seeds are no longer used. Separate land components remain separate;
students never cross water or an unsupported gap to reach a distant destination.
Student body colliders are triggers: they cannot push or physically block players.
Movement still checks solid scenery and separation from other characters.
Models hide during board/help/tutorial camera modes and restore their original
renderer visibility afterwards. The speaking student remains visible in dialogue.

INTERACTION
The normal hand dispatcher consumes a student conversation before firing gameplay
events; onboarding practice and required book/house interactions retain priority.
Talk requires proximity, facing and clear line of sight. A deliberate conversation
consumes the hand tap without dropping a carried item. Other inventory/placement
actions retain their existing behavior. Thought bubbles use the existing artwork,
actual hand icon, distance limit and bounded screen sizing.
Conversation uses one semi-transparent light-gray speech bubble projected above the NPC's head. There
is no parchment panel or Back button. The player can move and rotate the camera;
the trial timer follows its normal state. Only the speaker stops roaming.
Walking beyond talk range pops the bubble out, resumes roaming, and restores the
compact prompt under its usual proximity/hint rules. Repeated taps are consumed
without restarting the animation or generating extra hints. Other menus close
speech immediately. Screen-space sizing, wrapping, padding and edge placement
keep text legible; exceptionally long hints can scroll inside the bounded bubble.
Speech uses the model's head bone as its anchor, a heavier 36-point reading font,
and at most 36% of safe-area width (also capped by dialogueMaxWidth). One reversible
0.22-second pop-in / 0.16-second pop-out controller prevents overlapping tweens.

HINTS
Trial-specific partial adapters read the actual current question/action provider.
No validation, answer submission, question selection, progression or reward method
is called by NPC hints. Each skin has its own conversation style and casual lines.
The scene-local ledger records a hint before its dialogue opens, once per student
per challenge. It is independent of target activation or dialogue lifetime.
Afterwards, only casual dialogue is offered. Optional hint eligibility is rolled
once per skin/challenge. Yellow ! means a hint remains; nearby chat-only indicators
use an ellipsis without a no-hint disclaimer. Hints and the available-hint label
are blue; casual dialogue is black. All 60 supplied lines, plus emotion/personality
metadata, are stored in Resources/Students/StudentDialogueLines.json and keyed
by the exact skin ID. Line history survives NPC respawning within the trial and
excludes the previous line from each new random draw.

TUNING
Catalog: walking speed, collision capsule size, map spacing/cell budget, work per
frame, talk distance, clearance, rest interval and optional-hint probability.
Population policy: StudentNpcPolicy. Text: StudentConversation / StudentTrialState.

VERIFICATION
AssetAndPolicyChecks.txt: twelve prepared controllers, Idle/Walk, population policy,
skin exclusion and repeat-proof hint ledger.
SceneNavigationChecks.txt: saved scene collision sampling and actual Graph prefab
layouts. No scene is saved by this audit.
PlayModeChecks.txt: isolated offline Editor test, with real prepared controllers,
hand input and touch/UI raycasts. It does not load real trial controllers or saves.
The touch harness uses a temporary InputSettings clone so background desktop focus
cannot discard simulated taps. It restores the original settings afterwards.
The preview uses a separate Play Mode start scene. Open scene edits are kept, with
backup copies under Assets/Tests/Students/Backups when the open scene is unsaved.
Dialogue and bubble screenshots were inspected in the Editor sandbox.
Bubble checks cover all 60 supplied lines at 800x480, plus each skin's longest line
at 2160x1080 and 480x800. They check wrapping, safe-area bounds, face clearance,
hand input, rapid taps, range exit, roaming resume and the one-hint ledger.
Actual Android/iOS device performance remains a separate check.

Nothing has been committed or pushed.

OUTDOOR ROAMING AND BUBBLE UPDATE
NPC hand prompts retain the shared Book Statue artwork and actual hand icon.
Their bounded mobile screen size is enlarged 1.4x, with an 85%-safe-width cap.
Speech uses a 90%-opaque light-gray surface and matching tail, preserving black
casual text, blue hints and the existing padding/wrapping/one-hint ledger.

Truth Table and Rules of Inference use Walk for short trips and Run for longer
routes (22 world units by default). Speed eases from 3.2 to 6.4 units/second;
NPCs slow near destinations, players, sharper turns and elevation changes.
The existing per-skin Run clips and natural-cadence parameters are reused.
Other trials remain walk-only. No player speed, clips or controller assets change.
Trips mix short strolls with destinations in less-visited 20-unit map sectors.
Stairs are no longer treated as prohibited interaction points. The NPC collision
clearance and route sweep use the same configurable 0.7-unit step allowance.
Height-changing route links are checked in actual movement-sized substeps before
being added. NPCs continue to avoid water, body obstacles and interaction areas.
