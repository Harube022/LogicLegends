Foot-jitter regression — 2026-11-06

Cause
The recently added PlayerStrideWarp used the animated knee as its bend hint.
When stride extension crossed the hip-to-ankle line, that hint could reverse.
The ankle could still reach its target, so earlier reach/scale checks passed,
but the calf rotation changed sharply and shook vertices shared by calf/foot
skin weights. Near-straight knee locking and abrupt support-leg switching added
smaller pops.

Comparison
Used the same source clips, Generic retargeting, clip phases and playback data
with and without the old stride correction. Saved the original solver locally
in Library/FootJitterAudit/PlayerStrideWarp.before.txt. The new stride script is
uncommitted, so this was a preserved-code/native-clip comparison rather than a
branch switch. No Git checkout, reset, commit or push was performed.

Audit
- Global Animator and state base speeds are 1; running/walking use direct clip
  states, not blend trees. Root motion and Humanoid foot IK remain disabled.
- Carry masks affect the upper body; Generic retargeting runs before stride IK.
- No other project script was found driving the leg bones after stride IK.

Fix
- Stable forward/up/outward anatomical knee pole.
- Small near-extension reserve prevents knee locking/unlocking at singular reach.
- Continuous support handoff replaces a hard switch between the two legs.
- Clip assignments, playback/cadence values, movement speed, jump/stun timing,
  carrying poses, weights and physics remain unchanged.

Passed
- 24 matched-rate gait comparisons, 241 sampled poses each, observing both knee
  rotations and actual weighted sole vertices. Worst sampled knee step reduced
  from 178.9 to 15.2 degrees, with retained reach and ground clearance.
- All 12 skins in isolated Editor Play Mode: steady walking/running, state
  transitions, turns, carry/release, four weight levels, jump/landing and pause.
- Final LateUpdate monitoring after all pose writers: no reversed knee planes,
  both soles within ground tolerance, and enough samples for each movement mode.
- Existing cadence-clock, actual movement-speed and carry-alignment checks pass.
- All 300 original animation/FBX/importer hashes unchanged. No clips replaced.
