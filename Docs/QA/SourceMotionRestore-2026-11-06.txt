SOURCE-MOTION RESTORATION — 2026-11-06

Cause
The speed fixes introduced PlayerStrideWarp two-bone leg IK, stride enlargement,
pelvis lowering and dynamic ground corrections after Animator evaluation. This
changed authored motion to reconcile reduced cadence with unchanged travel speed.
The first solver also chose a pole from the animated knee, which could flip its
bend plane. A later stable-pole revision still widened the stride and was not a
restoration of the source motion. Those procedural corrections are now removed.
The donor-rig translation origin also used an animated Idle pose; mesh bind origins
now supply the fixed rig conversion, avoiding borrowed-gait vertical displacement.

Specific changes
- All 12 Gameplay prefabs: remove PlayerStrideWarp components and ground curves /
  sole correction data; retain their established uniform scale and controllers.
- PlayerStrideWarp: inert serialization compatibility only; no update or writes.
- PlayerAnimator: remove stride-ratio calculation; bounded Walk/Run playback only,
  smoothed over .18s. Global Animator speed stays 1, root motion stays off.
- SharedSkinAnimationRetarget: retain only necessary static bind-space conversion
  for explicitly requested donor states; remove phase/floor-following correction.
- LatestSkinPlaybackSetup and AnimationReuse setup: no stride fitting, ground
  calibration or procedural leg solver can be regenerated. Donor assignments and
  original animation assets are unchanged. Old playback/foot-jitter entry points
  route to source-motion checks rather than the removed solver.
- Carry upper-body pose/support, object alignment, all movement penalties and
  original movement/jump/stun behavior are retained. No gameplay scene was saved.

Verification and limits
All 12 skins passed isolated Editor Play Mode checks with real GameInput,
equipment selection, CharacterController movement, turns, state transitions,
walking, sustained running, carrying, jump/landing, drop, and all four existing
.95/.88/.80/.75 movement modifiers. The check compares both legs and feet plus
hips with the assigned clip at the same phase (after required donor bind-space
conversion), and monitors final-frame knee continuity including blends.
No limb translation change was measured; final lower-body angular differences
were at most about .44 degree, with hip differences under .00005 world unit.
Playback clocks advanced continuously without repeated state entry or duplicate
animation ticking. Static checks confirm only the nine requested donor states
changed, no Humanoid foot IK, no root motion and no hidden locomotion multiplier.
All 300 original animation / FBX / importer hashes match the pre-reuse baseline;
there are still 156 animation assets. No walking/running animations were created
or edited. No stride-component GUID references remain in scene or prefab files.
48 inherited player/resource/menu visuals passed read-only inspection.
Unity was restored to stopped, clean Formal vs. Informal Proofs; console no errors.
The isolated fixture does not run account, wallet, reward or progression code.
No commit, push, branch switch, merge or backend write was performed.

This verifies preservation of authored motion, not zero-slip foot locking. A short
in-place source gait slowed down at unchanged 13-unit walking / 19.5-unit running
can still slide relative to terrain. No wider strides, extra bone offsets or
hidden movement-speed change were added to conceal that constraint. Source
foot-height variation is retained. Physical Android and live network peers were
not tested. Review those environments separately after merging locally.
