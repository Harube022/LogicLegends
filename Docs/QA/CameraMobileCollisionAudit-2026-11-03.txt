Logic Legends — camera, mobile UI and collision audit
Date: 2026-11-03
Unity: 6000.3.6f1; Android is the selected build target.

Verification boundary
All runtime results below are Editor Play Mode results. Input System touch events,
pointer events and the real player CharacterController were used. ADB reported no
connected phone or emulator, so no actual Android build/device result is claimed.
Multiplayer checks were offline; live networking was not exercised.

Findings and fixes
1. Jump/hand/button touches could also drive pinch zoom. Shared touch routing now
   assigns camera gestures only when they begin on the look surface. Modal UI,
   tutorials and fixed board views cannot move the hidden gameplay camera.
2. A second finger could steal/release joystick or look drags. Each drag now has
   one pointer owner. Hidden controls, input blocking, pause and focus loss clear
   stale input, so closing a panel cannot resume an old drag.
3. Wrapped negative camera pitch could snap to the upper pitch limit. Initial
   and restored pitch now use a signed angle while retaining the authored limits.
4. Tutorial rigs could restore another active rig's camera settings, and callbacks
   could restart a tutorial before its previous camera was disabled. Shared
   camera leases and idempotent return cleanup preserve the original Brain state.
   Rigs also copy the rendered camera's clipping planes. Legacy manual camera
   updates defer to Cinemachine when its Brain controls that camera.
5. Board cleanup was incomplete when components were disabled. Formal/Informal,
   Sets and Trees now release their view/input ownership. Induction ignores
   repeated Back taps during return and resets the Brain after restoring its view.
6. Rules of Inference's board input guard used scaled time and could block Back
   during entry. The guard now uses real time; Back has an independent raycast
   group and remains available. Closing cancels the pending input-enable routine.
7. Trees Help appeared available while its board camera was still blending, but
   its handler rejected the tap. Both Help buttons now match that camera guard.
8. Repeated library book opens could overwrite the original enabled player state
   with the reading panel's disabled state. Duplicate opens are ignored; switching
   books first releases the previous reading state. Close remains idempotent.
9. Rules of Inference's stair support tried to read a non-readable imported mesh.
   It now samples the cooked collider instead. Imported assets need no Read/Write
   change, and support remains confined to the visible stairs/dry approaches.

  scripts and collider configuration. No missing scripts were found.

  PRELIM, TruthTable, RulesOfInference, Formal vs. Informal Proofs, Direct&Indirect,
  Induction, Setssss, Functions and Relations, Graph, and Trees.
- Main Menu: real simulated touches through Play and the local multiplayer
  choices/invite form, cancellation and Back at four landscape sizes.
- MultiplayerStage and MultiplayerGameplay: offline avatar, touch ownership,
  camera gestures, nested camera cleanup and floor/solid capsule sweeps.
- LogicGarden: hand practice and stable tutorial UI; embedded library tour,
  all replay options, No/Yes, Skip during a blend, reading and repeated travel.
  Double-open/double-close, switching books and disabled-controller cleanup passed.

Collision coverage
- Real player capsule downward and horizontal sweeps in all ten trials and both
  multiplayer scenes; movement-speed values were unchanged.
- Truth Table platform: nine continuous up/down joystick routes using three
  positions and three approach angles, adjacent grounding, jump and board checks.
- Truth Table paths: exact stone-mesh collision and grass grounding samples
  beside at least eight separate paths passed. All six house stairs were walked
  without jumping; closed doors blocked entry and opened entrances were accessible.
- Rules of Inference: four accessible stair approaches, without jumping; no ramp
  support over water beyond the stair footprint. Rear tower/water faces are not
  treated as walkable approaches.
- Inactive PRELIM pillar placeholders with empty mesh colliders have no visible
  geometry. No invisible collision walls were added to those placeholders.
- Trees: book collision from three sides, InformationBoard collision, 216
  perimeter samples and a continuous 17-waypoint chamber walk passed. Visible
  walls blocked the capsule; routes to the board and side aisles remained open.

Scope and integrity
Gameplay movement speeds, timer rules, validation, progression and intended UI
styling were retained. Checks used isolated/offline profiles and suppressed trial
persistence; they did not use real account rewards or purchases. Help recordings
were not replaced. Verification helpers are Editor-only.
No branches were switched. Nothing was committed or pushed.

Remaining verification
The broader Trees chamber helper also ran an unrelated lighting probe. Its
Reading Aisle Chandelier sample measured zero floor-brightness change. Lighting
was not changed in this audit; that sample needs a separate rendering/lighting
investigation. The camera/collision run excludes lamp photometry explicitly.

Run an Android device build on supported phones/tablets, including low frame rates,
OS pause/resume and notches. Validate live multiplayer separately. Editor checks
are not a substitute for those hardware/network checks or an exhaustive sweep of
every mesh from every possible approach angle.
