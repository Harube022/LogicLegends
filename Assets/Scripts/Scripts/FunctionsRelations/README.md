# Functions and Relations stage

Open `Assets/Scenes/Functions and Relations.unity`, or select stage 5 in the Main Menu. The stage uses the existing Player, GameInput, mobile controls, EventSystem, and Book Statue in that scene. `FR_GameplayCanvas` contains the mapping board; `FunctionsRelationsStageManager` holds the Inspector references. The editor command **Logic Legends → Configure Functions and Relations Stage** restores the board, node prefabs, crystal material assignment, and Build Settings entry if needed. **Logic Legends → Validate Functions and Relations Stage** checks the definitions and scene wiring. Completing stage 5 records stage 6 as unlocked through the existing progression method when a user is signed in.

The catalog at `Assets/Resources/FunctionsRelationsChallenges.asset` has five challenge groups with ten variants each. One variant from each group is chosen per playthrough, and a group cannot repeat its immediately previous variant on the next playthrough. The generator command **Logic Legends → Rebuild Functions and Relations Challenge Variants** recreates the 50 variants; it replaces edits to those variants, so use it only when changing the factory rules. Every problem offers Domain 1–4 and Range A–D, and Domain 4 and all four Range diamonds participate in every solution. The first two groups require four and five connections respectively. Challenge 3 now starts with one fixed laser: players infer the other three from the requirement to make a bijection and an alphabetical order clue. Challenge 4 has four preset lasers in every variant, so laser count cannot reveal the answer: five patterns are functions, while five repeat one input and leave another unmapped. Challenge 5 mixes bijections (all three properties) with surjective relations (Surjective only). `MappingRuleEvaluator` evaluates relations, functions, injective, surjective, bijective, and many-to-one mappings independently. The numbered Domain and Range diamonds have emitter and receiver anchors. `LaserMappingController` draws blue LineRenderer beams while a challenge is active, turns all beams green after a correct submission, and briefly turns all beams red after an incorrect submission. It never marks an individual wrong connection before confirmation.

Approach the Book Statue and press Interact to start the ten-minute shared timer. **View Board** smoothly frames the existing InformationBoard; **Back** restores the gameplay camera and the previous controls. Reading before activation does not start gameplay. During an active challenge, the countdown continues while viewing, as before. At Domain, choose an input (1–4); at Range, choose an output (A–D) or Undo a connection. **Submit** appears after creating a connection and checks the complete arrangement. In challenge 4, select the most precise category at the answer pedestals, then Submit. In challenge 5, toggle all applicable Injective, Surjective, and Bijective properties, then Submit. Wrong submissions preserve lasers and selections, deduct ten seconds, and play the existing red flash and error feedback. Correct submissions turn lasers green, pause the shared countdown, and unlock the next challenge. Interact with the book again to continue; it never submits answers or resets the countdown.

The diamond material reuses the existing `Crystal1_Base.png` texture with a transparent URP Lit material. The actual replacement gameplay art is still in progress; the existing domain and range meshes, UI, and audio are placeholders.

The existing `InformationBoard` prefab supplies the world challenge title, Domain/Codomain summary, and instructions; the old `Board` is disabled. **Logic Legends → Update Functions and Relations Presentation** reconnects the camera and UI references and applies PRELIM's panels, fonts, timer, and “Challenge X of Total” HUD without rebuilding scenery or unpacking prefabs. `FunctionsRelationsPresentation` arranges the HUD and choice panels inside the mobile safe area, with View Board, Submit, and contextual buttons clear of the controls. Hand and contextual interactions open the same selection panel anywhere within `diamondUseDistance` of the Domain/Range footprint, independent of camera aim. `FunctionsRelationsBoardView` blends the existing gameplay camera, temporarily suspends camera input, and restores camera, avatar visibility, cursor, and controls on Back or interruption. Number selection, Range Undo, Submit validation, and the countdown use the original gameplay controller.

The Mapping Chamber scenery is saved under `FR_Environment` in the scene. **Logic Legends → Configure Functions and Relations Environment** rebuilds only that visual hierarchy and its shared assets; it does not replace puzzle objects. It includes combined stone architecture and floor engravings, low-poly background mountains, five progression runes, and reusable `ArcanePylon` and `TempleTorch` prefabs. The `MappingChamberEnvironmentController` observes the stage manager's public state to brighten the Book rune on approach and activation and to illuminate one more rune after each completed challenge. It does not alter gameplay state. Its Inspector quality setting supports **Auto, Low, Medium, High**: Low removes ambient motes and distant floating ruins, Medium allows six motes, and High allows twelve motes and the distant ruins. Auto uses High in the Editor and Medium or Low on handheld devices based on reported graphics memory. All levels keep the same puzzle visibility and navigation.

The chamber now sits inside a continuous low-poly meadow with reused Simple Nature Pack trees, rocks, bushes, and grass. Its shared terrain MeshCollider catches the player just below the stone platform; four hidden colliders at the far, raised perimeter prevent walking off the landscape. Tree and rock colliders remain outside the puzzle routes. Low quality hides eight distant trees, while Medium and High show the full grove. The environment uses URP Lit/Unlit shared materials, the existing nature atlas, a procedural sky, distant linear fog, and one existing directional light. It adds no real-time lights or post-processing. The combined meshes and prefabs are editable in the scene or via the environment builder. Rebuild only after preserving any manual changes made inside `FR_Environment`.

`FunctionsRelationsHelpUI` opens How to Play after the local movement controller is ready. It uses PRELIM's parchment, fonts, paged navigation, and Help button. Help captures/restores input, camera input, cursor, player control, and time scale; an active challenge and its connections remain unchanged. The guide reads the configured countdown/penalty and describes the five actual challenge types, including the later answer/property pedestals.

On first onboarding, Let's Begin launches `FunctionsRelationsControlsOnboarding`. Deliberate joystick movement, a successful jump, and a practice hand interaction lead to guided Book, timer, InformationBoard/Back, Domain, output/codomain, Undo, and Submit steps. Practice values 6/9 and X/Y, labels, and a separate laser use the existing station UI callbacks without loading a challenge or modifying the real mapping. One shared PRELIM Skip cancels the camera and practice content before the same Let's start? confirmation. No resets movement practice; Yes restores controls and clears only the onboarding gate, leaving the book to start Challenge 1. Reopening Help during gameplay closes back to that gameplay state.

Manual checks before release: run on the target phone aspect ratios; complete and skip onboarding at several steps; use No then Yes; reopen Help with an active timer; use the joystick and Interact at each diamond and pedestal; confirm the Book Statue requires proximity; submit wrong answers with and without lasers; let the timer expire; retry; and play all five challenges through the results screen. Check laser visibility, pedestal labels, and crystal appearance on the intended mobile graphics quality settings.
