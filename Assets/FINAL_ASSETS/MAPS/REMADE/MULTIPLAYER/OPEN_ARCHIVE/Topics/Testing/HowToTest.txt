QUICK START

1. Press Play. The original movement, camera, jump, water and interaction controls remain in use.
2. Expand the monster test tools using Show / hide on the left.
3. While offline and the monster is sleeping, choose Previous Topic / Next Topic, then Visit Topic.
4. Approach the open-book lectern. Use the original Hand button or E to start and read the challenge.
5. Close the reader to use the world objects. Reopen the book whenever needed. Reading does not pause the world or grant protection.
6. Complete the shared answer and confirm through Submit in the reader. Propositional Logic instead requires channeling the chosen world rune.
7. Correct answers unlock the next round at that topic's book. A wrong answer retains the work, records the responsible actor and advances the existing monster awakening mistake count once.

ACTIVITIES

T1  Rune Choice Circle: identify a connective by channeling one of four ground runes.
T2  Truth Mosaic: carry replenishing True/False tokens into four truth-table row sockets.
T3  Inference Constellation: fill premise connectors, then carry the matching rule crystal to its altar. The conclusion appears after a correct rule.
T4  Scribes' Court: classify proof scrolls, order proof statements, supply missing rules, and pair statements with justifications.
T5  Proof Forge: identify direct/contrapositive/contradiction reasoning and arrange proof tablets.
T6  Induction Court: place formula scrolls into Base, Hypothesis and ordered Induction Step sockets.
T7  Garden of Sets: carry number blocks into membership, intersection, difference and complement regions. Five challenges, with two problems each (10 total).
T8  Mapping Conduit: physically visit an input and output to connect them; classify functions and select mapping properties.
T9  Path of Connections: the selected walker must travel continuously along the displayed graph connections. Retry Route clears only the route attempt. Degree questions use number markers.
T10 Tree of Connections: identify nodes, connect/remove branches, complete spanning trees, and answer depth/height questions.

There are 59 rounds total, with five additional premise validations in T3. Each Sets challenge draws two distinct problems from its existing question bank. Prop positions and proof/formula labels shuffle deterministically for the room.

TEAM RULES

All players share one answer and one progress record per topic. Each player can hold one item at a time. True/False tokens replenish so multiple players can use separate copies.

Commands use the Photon event sender, station revision, actor sequence, distance, owned player and capture/health eligibility checks. Disconnected players return held props; their mistake identities and saved names remain in the team record. Late join and authority changes retain team work.

Existing capture/barrier/health outcomes and monster hunting rotation remain controlled by the existing encounter. Captured players cannot contribute to the topic challenges; other eligible players remain free.

Development Visit Topic and monster swapping are offline review tools. Visit is unavailable during hunting or capture.
