# Open Archive surface art pass

Applied to the existing MultiplayerGameplay, exploration, awakening, hunting and cinematic test scenes on 19 October 2026. Open a saved scene normally; the materials and decoration are already assigned. No runtime setup is required.

## Flicker and overlapping-face repair

The audit found repeated bridge modules drawing the same end posts and caps, overlapping individual paving pieces, coplanar spawn/nest footings, and overlapping small surface decorations. `Editor/OpenArchiveFlickerRepair.cs` now clips only the redundant visible faces, preserving their material/UV attributes and the complete visible surface coverage. All four bridges and twenty-one affected surface renderers are repaired in each of the five scenes; the reusable complete bridge prefab is repaired too. The standalone railing module retains both end posts for normal reuse. The existing collider meshes, navigation data, transforms, layers, static flags, player/monster components and LOD settings remain unchanged. New meshes are render-only assets under `Meshes/StableSurfaces`; `Authoring/FlickerSources.json` retains their original visual sources for repeatable authoring.

The tree/timber and natural-surface shaders now compile the native URP soft-shadow variants. All three shared environment shaders use URP's normal shadow-distance fade, avoiding the abrupt moving shadow boundary from the earlier one-argument shadow lookup. Fine grain relief is slope-limited and fades when its texture becomes smaller than a pixel. Tree detail uses continuous model-space projections on both LODs and explicit texture gradients, avoiding a change of texture phase/projection as the camera moves. Existing bark, leaf, needle and wood-grain art remains. These changes keep the original two-sample MSAA, 1024px shadow atlas, camera clip planes, quality settings and pipeline asset; there is no new runtime rendering controller, post effect, light or particle system.

Use **Tools > Logic Legends > Open Archive > Art > Repair overlapping faces and flicker** after older geometry or art setup commands. Save open edits and stop Play Mode first. The command saves all five scenes, validates original gameplay/physics state, and restores the previously open scene. For a full reapplication of tree textures, run the tree/bridge art command first and this repair last. Do not assign the stable render meshes to MeshColliders or regenerate the navigation bake for this repair.

Evidence: `Reports/FlickerGeometryRepair.json` passes scene and bridge-prefab preservation checks. `FlickerOverlapAudit.json` audits approximately 305,000 LOD0 inventory triangles: 5,346 exact same-facing duplicate bridge triangles decrease to zero, and all significant horizontal surface overlaps are removed. Intentional opposite-facing foliage remains. Two unchanged numerical boundary contacts each have projected areas below 0.00011 local square units. The audited inventory decreases by 968 triangles overall despite additional cut boundaries; this count is not an in-frame GPU benchmark. `FlickerShaderChecks.json` confirms shader support/compilation and unchanged camera/graphics settings. `FlickerSourceProtection.json` records protected source assets. No original FBX, collision mesh, navigation asset, health, input, networking or encounter script was changed.

`Reports/FlickerMotionReview.json` records 72 moving-camera renders in actual Play Mode using the existing exploration camera lens and 2x MSAA: bridge posts/boards, trees, nest entrance and spawn. Each sweep isolates camera-dependent artifacts with consecutive renders in one Play Mode frame. Native soft-shadow filtering is active and no Unity errors were present. `Previews/Flicker_Bridge_Review.gif`, `Flicker_Trees_Review.gif`, `Flicker_Nest_Review.gif` and `Flicker_Spawn_Review.gif` contain these camera sweeps. Android device behavior and live multiplayer were not tested; no finite set of views can guarantee every possible device/camera situation.

## Tree bark, canopy and bridge timber follow-up

All 214 existing trees now have visible bark grain and canopy detail on both original LODs. Broadleaf crowns use the project's painted leaf silhouettes and veins; the two pine models have a separate needle mask. Trunk, branch and leaf colors still come from the original NaturePack palette. Bridge planks, posts and diagonal braces use the existing painted `Wood3.tga` grain with worn grooves, subtle relief and end grain. Grain follows the long direction of each timber piece, including angled braces. The bridge's original board gaps, rail positions and clearance remain.

`Authoring/pack_tree_bridge_detail.py` packs the existing timber and leaf artwork plus data masks into two shared 512px RGBA maps. Source textures remain untouched. Both maps use linear sampling, mipmaps, trilinear filtering, fourfold anisotropy and Android ASTC 6x6 (approximately 0.30 MiB combined with mipmaps). The opaque URP shader adds no transparent cards, geometry, renderers, lights, particles, colliders or runtime scripts. Thirteen render-only mesh copies add a third UV channel for texture coordinates; positions, triangles, normals, original palette UVs and vertex colors are unchanged. These coordinates survive Unity's static batching. Original FBX models and all collider meshes are retained.

Use **Tools > Logic Legends > Open Archive > Art > Apply tree bark, leaves and bridge timber** after any older map or surface generators. Save edits and stop Play Mode first. The command updates all five scenes, the five Archive_Tree prefab variants, and both bridge prefabs, then restores the previously open scene. The shared `Archive_TexturedTrees` and `Archive_TexturedBridgeWood` materials expose texture strength, repeat sizes, bark brightness, tint and fine relief. Pine detail has its own repeat size.

Evidence: `Reports/TreeBridgePreservation.json` passes all five scenes and seven prefabs, including transform/layer/static flags, original physics, gameplay components, LOD groups and unrelated renderers. `TreeBridgeAssetChecks.json` checks both texture imports, shader compilation and all thirteen derived mesh coordinate streams. `TreeBridgeSourceProtection.json` compares 784 pre-task files: only the five scenes and seven intended prefabs changed. `TreeBridgePlayReview.json` records 508 textured renderer slots, 214 LOD groups, actual LOD0/LOD1 renders and intact texture coordinates after native static batching. No Unity errors were present. Android hardware and live multiplayer were not tested; no APK build was started. Gameplay movement tests were not repeated for this material/UV-only change.

## Natural waterside and flowing-water follow-up

The four small bank cascades now use a dedicated downward-flowing waterfall material instead of the flat pond material. All eight outer cascades use the same animated ribbon shader, with their visual sheets brought clear of the uneven cliff face. Each waterfall has a small, fading impact-foam patch with moving ripples and native mesh-particle spray. Pond ripples are irregular and flow continuously, with broken-up shoreline foam. Water stays opaque and the falling sheets use clipped edges; only the twelve small impact patches use transparency. No camera depth/color copies, reflections, lights or colliders were added.

Natural boulders and bank stones have continuous gray stone grain, fine relief, damp waterline shading and restrained moss. Render-only mesh copies smooth the harsh triangular shading and remove the alternating palette swatches. Combined bushes now use a dedicated leaf material rather than the stone atlas material. Their leaf shading is softer, with green leaf detail; the original bark, grass and flower palette cells remain. Original vertex positions, triangles and physics meshes are preserved. The ruins, cliffs and paving retain their existing architectural texture treatment.

Use **Tools > Logic Legends > Open Archive > Art > Apply natural rocks, foliage and waterfall polish** to reapply this pass, after any older environment/art generators. Stop Play Mode and save open edits first. `Editor/OpenArchiveWatersideArt.cs` updates the five existing scenes and restores the previously open scene. There is no new runtime controller. Tune stone/leaf grain, relief, repeat size and moss on the `Archive_NaturalRiverStone`, `Archive_WeatheredNaturalBank` and `Archive_SoftLeafClusters` materials; the source-scale scene has corresponding material variants. `Archive_AnimatedCascades` exposes flow speed, color, foam and sheet motion. `Prefabs/Archive_WaterfallImpact.prefab` exposes spray settings and the shared impact-foam material.

`Authoring/pack_waterside_masks.py` reproduces two 256px packed data textures: flow/foam noise and neutral stone/leaf detail. The source rock texture is read only. Both maps use linear sampling, mipmaps, trilinear filtering and Android ASTC 6x6 (about 0.08 MiB combined including mipmaps). The nine waterfall renderers contain 2,752 triangles in total. Twelve added particle systems have a combined cap of 120 small opaque droplets and reuse the existing FullOpaqueFire octahedron mesh. The earlier fire/spawn effects remain, for a combined maximum of 320 particles in these environment effects. Native-device frame time and overdraw have not been profiled.

## Clearer stone texture follow-up

Ruins, nest pillars, rubble, cliffs and paths now use a shared 512px neutral rock-grain/crevice texture derived from the existing `T_YFRM_05_d.tga`. Their original palette and low-poly normals remain. Paving has stronger, antialiased mortar joints, a small bevel highlight and clearer variation between stones. The texture strength is .82 on stone/cliffs and .64 on paving, so the paths remain readable. Tune `_StoneMap`, `_StoneTextureStrength` and `_TileMeters` in the existing shared materials; **Tools > Logic Legends > Open Archive > Art > Apply visible stone textures** restores these assignments without editing scenes or geometry. `Authoring/pack_stone_detail.py` records and reproduces the derived texture.

The additional map uses mipmaps, trilinear filtering, fourfold anisotropy, no CPU-readable copy and Android ASTC 6x6 (about 0.15 MiB including mipmaps). Only stone surfaces sample it; it adds no render pass, transparent surface, collider or light. `Previews/Stone_After.png` shows the actual saved scene materials; `Stone_RaisedCapture.png` shows the player caught on the pillar footing with the production barrier active. `Reports/StoneTextureChecks.json` records passing material/import/shader checks; Android hardware performance remains unverified. `ElevatedReachSourceProtection.json` confirms 238 protected files remain unchanged, with only the two existing hunting files intentionally edited for the related raised-rock reach correction.

## Grass, fire, spawn and overlap follow-up

The green ground now also samples the existing project's full painted grass albedo, copied at 512px with mipmaps and Android ASTC 6x6. Its color detail is normalized against the texture's measured linear average, retaining the original ground and clearing colors while making the grass pattern much more visible. `_GrassTextureStrength`, `_GrassMap` and repeat size are exposed on Archive_Meadow.

All eight torch bowls now have looping faceted flame particles and rising embers. The old static flame triangles are removed from a separate render-only torch mesh; original pedestal geometry assets and colliders remain unchanged. The reusable `Prefabs/Archive_TorchFire.prefab` uses the existing FullOpaqueFire octahedron mesh with a URP particle material, not that package's original VFX controller or lighting setup.

`Prefabs/Archive_SpawnWelcome.prefab` adds two counter-rotating, gently pulsing green/turquoise arcs and rising welcome motes at the existing spawn glyph. These are visual effects only. Native ParticleSystems and shader time animate them automatically in Play Mode. No movement, teleport, input, networking or spawn behavior was added. Seventeen native particle systems share a maximum budget of 200 opaque mesh particles; only the two narrow halo strips use transparency. No lights or colliders were added.

The nest entrance flicker was caused by the dark floor and all four stone landings sharing the same plane. `NestFloor_Visual_EntranceCutouts` removes the hidden floor areas beneath those landings: measured overlap decreases from 88.48 local square meters to zero. Thin bed, buried-tile and claw-mark surfaces also have separate visual meshes with modest, ordered height separation. The original MeshCollider meshes, baked navigation data, transform positions and MonsterSpawnPoint are untouched. This is an approved change to render-mesh references, superseding the earlier pass's unchanged-render-reference check for those specific objects.

Apply or repair these changes through **Tools > Logic Legends > Open Archive > Art > Apply grass, fire, spawn and surface repair**, with Play Mode stopped and open edits saved. Apply it after using the older geometry generators. The effects' size, color, lifetime, emission and particle caps are editable in their shared prefab assets; halo color, pulse and spin are material properties.

Evidence: `Reports/FireSpawnSurfaceRepair.json` covers all five saved scenes; `PolishSourceProtection.json` confirms original asset hashes; `FireSpawnPlayMode.json` confirms automatic particle movement, shader animation time, controls, fourteen complete monster routes and a sixteen-frame moving-camera landing review. `Previews/Polish_Review.png` shows the results and `Polish_LandingMotion.gif` shows the camera sweep. ## Reuse and tuning

The material detail comes from existing project textures: the painted Grass_normal and dirt_desatured_rocks assets, plus softened grain from T_YFRM_01_d. The originals are not modified. `Authoring/pack_surface_details.py` packs their neutral detail into one padded 1024px RGBA atlas and a 128px variation map. Fine cracks, wood grain and moss masks are generated as data. `Reports/TextureSources.json` records the source paths.

`Materials/ArchiveDetailedSurface.shader` combines the existing palette or vertex colors with world-space detail. This supports the kit's palette-point UVs without changing mesh channels or stretching a single color swatch. Textures use mipmaps, trilinear filtering, twofold anisotropy, no CPU-readable copy, and Android ASTC 6x6 overrides. The added detail textures require approximately 0.61 MiB with ASTC and mipmaps. Materials share these maps, support instancing and the SRP Batcher, and have forward, shadow and depth passes. Existing batching flags and tree LODs remain intact.

Inspector controls on the surface materials expose repeat size in meters, detail strength, moss, palette tint and vertex-color use. Water exposes foam tint/strength, wave height and flow speed. Edit shared material assets to update all five scenes. The shader intentionally uses the main directional light and ambient illumination rather than per-pixel additional lights.

Lighting uses warm afternoon sun, ambient sky/ground colors and slight distance fog. The active Very Low preset now allows inexpensive hard shadows; the existing 1024px, one-cascade URP shadow map and 25m shadow distance are retained. No new realtime lights, post-processing, transparent vegetation cards, tessellation or parallax effects were added. Verify this modest shadow cost on target phones; lower presets can disable shadows if required.

Authoring commands are under **Tools > Logic Legends > Open Archive > Art**:

- **Apply surface and daylight upgrade** updates the five saved scenes in place, validates original transforms/components/colliders/mesh references, then restores the previously open scene. Stop Play Mode and save edits first. It does not invoke the map generator or rebake navigation.
- **Render upgraded scene** captures actual scene geometry under the current quality settings.

The earlier full environment/den rebuilding commands are authoring history and can reset material assignments. Reapply this surface pass after using them. Do not regenerate the map for routine texture tuning.
